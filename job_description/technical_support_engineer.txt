Technical Support Engineer (Entry Level)

Company: SupportPro Services

Job Description:
We are looking for a Technical Support Engineer to provide technical assistance and support for incoming queries and issues related to computer systems, software, and hardware.

Responsibilities:
- Respond to customer queries via phone, email, or chat
- Diagnose and resolve technical hardware and software issues
- Follow up with customers to ensure issues are resolved
- Document technical knowledge in the form of notes and manuals
- Escalate unresolved issues to higher-level support

Requirements:
- Bachelor's degree in IT, Computer Science, or related field
- Basic understanding of computer systems and troubleshooting
- Strong communication and customer service skills
- Ability to work in shifts if required
- Eagerness to learn and adapt
