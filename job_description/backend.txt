Backend Developer

Company: DataDrive Inc.

Job Description:
We are seeking a Backend Developer to design, build, and maintain the server-side logic and databases for our web applications. You will ensure high performance and responsiveness to requests from the front-end.

Responsibilities:
- Develop and maintain server-side logic, APIs, and databases
- Integrate user-facing elements with server-side logic
- Optimize applications for maximum speed and scalability
- Implement security and data protection measures
- Collaborate with front-end developers and other team members
- Write reusable, testable, and efficient code

Requirements:
- Bachelor's degree in Computer Science or related field
- Proficiency in backend languages (Python, Java, Node.js, etc.)
- Experience with frameworks like Django, Express, or Spring
- Strong understanding of databases (SQL, NoSQL)
- Familiarity with RESTful API design
- Knowledge of version control (Git)
- Good problem-solving and communication skills
