Product Management Intern

Company: BrightIdeas Tech

Job Description:
We are looking for a Product Management Intern to support our product team in researching, planning, and launching new features. This is an ideal role for someone interested in product strategy and user experience.

Responsibilities:
- Conduct market and user research
- Assist in defining product requirements
- Support product managers in project coordination
- Help track product metrics and feedback
- Prepare presentations and reports

Requirements:
- Bachelor's degree in Business, Engineering, or related field (or pursuing)
- Strong interest in product management and technology
- Analytical and organizational skills
- Good communication and teamwork abilities
- Eagerness to learn and contribute
