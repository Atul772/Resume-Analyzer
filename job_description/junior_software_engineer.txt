Junior Software Engineer

Company: NextGen Solutions

Job Description:
We are looking for a motivated Junior Software Engineer to join our development team. You will assist in designing, coding, and testing software applications under the guidance of senior engineers.

Responsibilities:
- Assist in the development and maintenance of software applications
- Write clean, efficient, and well-documented code
- Participate in code reviews and team meetings
- Debug and troubleshoot software issues
- Collaborate with cross-functional teams
- Learn and apply new technologies as needed

Requirements:
- Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, IT, or related field
- Familiarity with at least one programming language (Python, Java, C++, etc.)
- Understanding of software development fundamentals
- Eagerness to learn and grow in a team environment
- Good problem-solving and communication skills
