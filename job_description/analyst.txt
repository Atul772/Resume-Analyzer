Analyst

Company: Business Insights Group

Job Description:
We are seeking an Analyst to interpret data, analyze results, and provide ongoing reports to support business decisions. You will work with large datasets and present findings to stakeholders.

Responsibilities:
- Analyze data using statistical techniques
- Develop and maintain dashboards and reports
- Identify trends and patterns in complex data sets
- Work with management to prioritize business needs
- Prepare presentations and communicate insights
- Ensure data integrity and quality

Requirements:
- Bachelor's degree in Business, Economics, Statistics, or related field
- Proficiency in Excel, SQL, and data visualization tools (Power BI, Tableau)
- Strong analytical and problem-solving skills
- Attention to detail and accuracy
- Good communication and presentation skills
