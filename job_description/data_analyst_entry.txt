Data Analyst (Entry Level)

Company: DataBridge Analytics

Job Description:
We are seeking a Data Analyst to collect, process, and perform basic analysis on data to support business decisions. This is an entry-level position ideal for recent graduates.

Responsibilities:
- Collect and clean data from various sources
- Perform basic data analysis and generate reports
- Visualize data using charts and dashboards
- Assist in identifying trends and patterns
- Present findings to team members

Requirements:
- Bachelor's degree in Statistics, Mathematics, Computer Science, or related field
- Proficiency in Excel and basic knowledge of SQL
- Familiarity with data visualization tools (Power BI, Tableau) is a plus
- Analytical mindset and attention to detail
- Good communication skills
