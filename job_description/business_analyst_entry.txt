Business Analyst (Entry Level)

Company: Visionary Analytics

Job Description:
We are looking for an entry-level Business Analyst to help analyze business processes, gather requirements, and support project teams in delivering solutions.

Responsibilities:
- Assist in gathering and documenting business requirements
- Analyze data and business processes
- Support project managers and stakeholders
- Prepare reports and presentations
- Participate in meetings and brainstorming sessions

Requirements:
- Bachelor's degree in Business, Economics, or related field
- Strong analytical and problem-solving skills
- Proficiency in MS Office (Excel, PowerPoint)
- Good communication and teamwork abilities
- Eagerness to learn and adapt
