Junior UI/UX Designer

Company: Creative Minds Studio

Job Description:
We are seeking a Junior UI/UX Designer to help design user interfaces and improve user experiences for our digital products. You will work closely with senior designers and developers.

Responsibilities:
- Assist in designing wireframes, mockups, and prototypes
- Conduct user research and usability testing
- Collaborate with developers to implement designs
- Maintain design consistency and brand guidelines
- Learn and apply UI/UX best practices

Requirements:
- Bachelor's degree in Design, Computer Science, or related field (or pursuing)
- Basic knowledge of design tools (Figma, Adobe XD, Sketch)
- Creative thinking and attention to detail
- Good communication and teamwork skills
- Eagerness to learn and grow
